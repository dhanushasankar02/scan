Common Questions Before the Demo

Don't worry if you don't have all the answers yet. That's what the demo is for. Here are a few things people usually ask us first.

How long does setup take?

We can typically have your menu digitized and your QR codes printed within 24-48 hours of receiving your menu.

Do I need special hardware?

No. ScanDine runs entirely in the browser. Your guests use their own phones, and you can view the kitchen dashboard on any tablet or computer you already own.

Can I integrate with my current POS?

Yes! We integrate directly with Toast, Square, Clover, and many other major POS systems to sync your orders seamlessly.
