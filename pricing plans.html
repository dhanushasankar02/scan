Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing, plans, and billing. Can't find the answer you're looking for? Contact our support team.

Can I change my plan later?
Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your admin dashboard. If you upgrade, the changes take effect immediately. If you downgrade, the changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there a setup fee?
No. We believe in transparent pricing. There are no setup fees, hidden charges, or long-term contracts. You only pay the monthly subscription.
Do you offer a free trial?
Yes! All plans come with a 14-day free trial. You do not need to enter a credit card to start your trial.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) as well as Apple Pay and Google Pay.
